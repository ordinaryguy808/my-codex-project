# Tappr Real Estate Launch — Concept Board Guardrails

The attached/generated Tappr product catalog concept board is a **visual reference only**.

Use it to guide:
- product appearance
- premium real-estate positioning
- black, white, and purple brand direction
- product-photography style
- packaging presentation
- visual hierarchy
- ecommerce look and feel

Do **not** treat specifications shown in the concept board as verified product facts.

Unless separately verified before fulfillment, do not publish as confirmed:
- waterproof or weatherproof claims
- UV or scratch resistance
- exact dimensions or thickness
- exact material composition
- outdoor lifespan
- NFC read distance
- manufacturing method
- packaging dimensions or materials
- production tolerances

When visual concepts conflict with written product requirements, the written product requirements control.

Current website product and packaging visuals should therefore be described as early-access concepts, not final production specifications.
