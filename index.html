Your card uses NFC—the same contactless technology as tap-to-pay. Hold it near a compatible phone and your chosen link opens instantly.

No. Tappr works with the web browser already on their phone, so there is nothing to download.

Yes, update your destination anytime from your dashboard without replacing the card.
