How does a Tappr device work?+

A compatible phone can read the device and open the destination selected for its Tap ID.

Do my customers need an app?+

No. Tappr works with the web browser already on their phone, so there is nothing to download.

Can I change where my Tap Device links to?+

Yes. The destination can be updated later without replacing the physical Tappr device.

Which phones does it work with?+

Tappr works with most modern NFC-enabled iPhone and Android devices.
