FAQ

Have questions? We've got you.

Here's how Tappr works for agents, listings, and open houses.

Tappr provides marketing technology and physical products. Real-estate professionals remain responsible for brokerage policies, seller authorization, MLS requirements, advertising disclosures, trademark permissions, and applicable sign rules.

What exactly is Tappr?+

Tappr connects your physical marketing to the information you want clients to see online. Someone taps or scans your Tappr, and their phone opens the page you've selected.

Do buyers need to download an app?+

No. There's nothing for them to install. They can tap with a compatible phone or scan the QR code and continue in their normal browser.

Why does Tappr include both NFC and QR?+

They work well together. NFC makes close-up interactions quick and easy, while QR gives visitors another way to open the same information when tapping isn't convenient.

Can I use Tappr for a property listing?+

Yes. You can send visitors to a public listing page, property website, virtual tour, digital brochure, agent page, or another web destination you're authorized to use.

Can I reuse it after the property sells?+

Yes. Instead of replacing the product because a URL changed, the destination assigned to the Tap ID can be updated for your next listing.

Can I use one Tappr for several properties at once?+

One Tappr currently sends visitors to one destination at a time. You can use separate Tap IDs for different properties or send visitors to a page containing several listings.

Can I use it at an open house?+

Absolutely. Place the Open House Stand where guests can easily reach it and send them to property details, a contact page, lead form, or another useful destination.

How close does someone need to be to tap?+

NFC is designed for close-range use. Visitors should bring their phone close to the Tappr area. For greater distance, they can use the QR code.

What if someone's phone doesn't support NFC?+

They can scan the QR code instead.

Does Tappr need Wi-Fi or batteries?+

The physical NFC product does not need a battery. The visitor's phone does need an internet connection to load the Tappr page and final destination.

Can I use Tappr on a metal sign?+

Metal can interfere with ordinary NFC tags, so the product must use components appropriate for the intended mounting surface. Tappr will confirm the setup before fulfillment.

Is the Smart Sign Rider weatherproof?+

Tappr is developing the property-sign product for outdoor use, but final weather resistance depends on the materials, finish, and mounting method selected. Final specifications will be confirmed before fulfillment.

What analytics do I get?+

Tappr currently supports basic interaction counts and timestamps. More advanced analytics may be developed as the platform grows.

Can Tappr tell me who tapped it?+

A normal tap does not identify the person. You can direct visitors to an existing lead form, booking page, or CRM form if you want them to provide their information.

Can I use my brokerage logo?+

Yes, as long as you have permission to use it and your brokerage allows the design.

Can I put REALTOR® on my product?+

Only if you're authorized to use the REALTOR® membership marks and your use follows the applicable trademark rules.

Does Tappr make my real-estate advertising compliant?+

Tappr provides the technology and physical product. Agents remain responsible for brokerage policies, seller authorization, MLS requirements, advertising disclosures, and applicable sign rules.

Can I change my destination myself?+

During early access, Tappr manages destination changes for customers. A self-service customer dashboard is not currently promised.

Is there a monthly fee?+

There is no separate monthly fee listed for the current early-access purchase. Future hosting, analytics, or software pricing has not been finalized.

Do I pay when I submit the customization form?+

No. The current form is a request, not checkout. Tappr reviews the product, design, destination, and fulfillment details before confirming an order.
